# SOP-S03 Fatigue and Drowsiness
Operators showing signs of drowsiness (eyes closing, head nodding, delayed reactions) must park the machine safely at the first opportunity.
Park on level ground, lower all attachments, apply the parking brake, and shut down the engine. Notify the supervisor by radio.
A minimum 15-minute break is required after a drowsiness alert. Two drowsiness alerts in one shift require supervisor assessment before continuing.
Maximum shift length is 12 hours with a 30-minute break every 4 hours.
