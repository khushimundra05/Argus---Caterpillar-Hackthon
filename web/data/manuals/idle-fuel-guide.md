# Guide F01 Idle Reduction and Fuel Efficiency
Idle fuel burn: CAT 320 excavator approx 3.8 litres per hour; CAT 950 GC wheel loader approx 4.5 L/h; CAT D6 dozer approx 5.2 L/h.
Shut the engine down if waiting more than 5 minutes. Enable the engine idle shutdown timer and auto engine speed control.
Idle hours count toward service intervals: excessive idling increases maintenance cost and wear on the engine, turbo and DPF.
Coordinate with the truck dispatcher to reduce waiting time between loads.
