# SOP-S01 Seatbelt and ROPS
The seatbelt must be fastened before the engine is started and remain fastened whenever the machine is running, including short repositioning moves.
If the seat switch reports the belt unfastened while the engine is running, the operator must stop, apply the parking brake, lower attachments to the ground, and fasten the belt before resuming.
Inspect the seatbelt at every pre-start check: webbing for cuts or fraying, buckle latch and release, and anchor bolts. Replace the belt every 3 years or immediately if damaged.
Never operate a machine with a damaged ROPS or seatbelt. Tag the machine out of service and notify the supervisor.
