# Checklist P01 Pre-start Walkaround
Walk around the machine counter-clockwise starting at the left front. Check for leaks under the machine, damaged hoses, track or tyre condition, bucket teeth and pins.
Check engine oil, hydraulic oil and coolant levels with the machine on level ground. Drain water from the fuel water separator.
Use three points of contact when mounting and dismounting. Never jump from the machine.
In the cab: fasten seatbelt, adjust mirrors, test horn, travel alarm, lights and cameras before moving.
Warm up the hydraulic system for 5 minutes in cold weather below 0 degrees C.
Wet conditions: reduce travel speed, avoid side slopes over 15 degrees, and allow extra stopping distance. Rain typically increases cycle time by 15 to 25 percent.
