# SOP-S02 Hazard Zones and Ground Personnel
The exclusion zone is the full swing radius of the machine plus 1 metre. No ground personnel may enter the exclusion zone while the machine is operating.
If a person is detected within 3 metres, the operator must stop all machine motion immediately, lower the bucket or blade, and neutralise hydraulic controls.
Between 3 and 6 metres, the operator must slow down, sound the horn, and establish eye contact or radio contact before continuing.
Blind spots: rear and right-rear for excavators; rear for wheel loaders and dozers. Use rear-view camera and mirrors before reversing.
Spotters must use agreed hand signals. If the signal is unclear, stop.
